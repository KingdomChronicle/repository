Daily Devotional Verses



Proverbs 11:10

NKJV (New King James Version)

The Folly of Wickedness

When it goes well with the righteous, the city rejoices; And when the wicked perish, there is jubilation.

GUIDE REFLECTION:

The Life of the Righteous Becomes a Blessing to Others

The way we live affects more than just ourselves. Our words, choices, attitudes, abilities, and everything God has entrusted to us can have a meaningful impact on the people around us.

Solomon brings this truth before us in this verse:

“When it goes well with the righteous, the city rejoices; And when the wicked perish, there is jubilation.”

When someone who lives righteously prospers, that person is not the only one who benefits. His wealth, position, abilities, and influence can be used to help others. In this way, his flourishing can become a source of joy and blessing to the people around him.

On the other hand, when someone who uses power to exploit, oppress, harm, or deceive others is no longer able to continue doing evil, those who suffered because of him experience relief.

The point of the verse is not simply that people rejoice over the downfall of the wicked. Rather, it shows how far-reaching the influence of a person's life can be. Some people prosper in ways that bless those around them, while others leave people relieved when their ability to do harm finally comes to an end.

👉 True blessing is measured not only by what we receive, but also by the good others experience because of the way we live.

Righteousness Is Never Just Personal

Sometimes we think of righteousness as something purely personal—being honest, doing what is right, and obeying God. But this verse reminds us that righteousness also affects our families, workplaces, churches, and communities.

A just leader becomes a blessing to those under his care. When he refuses bribes, does not abuse his authority, and stands up for the weak, many people benefit from his integrity.

In the same way, a faithful parent sets a godly example for his children. A fair employer becomes a blessing to the families who depend on their work. A trustworthy friend becomes a source of strength and support in times of difficulty.

The blessings God gives us, then, are not meant to stop with us. They can flow through our lives and become a blessing to others.

👉 The blessings God entrusts to us become even more meaningful when we use them to serve and bless others.

What Effect Does Our Life Have on Others?

There is an important question worth asking:

What do other people experience because I am part of their lives?

When we have money, authority, position, or influence, we can choose to use it only for ourselves. We can use our words to elevate ourselves while putting others down. We can continually place our own desires, reputation, and interests ahead of everyone else.

Sometimes the problem is not obvious wickedness. It may simply be a heart that has become deeply centered on self. While we focus on our own advancement, we may fail to notice that others are being hurt, neglected, or even used as a means to achieve what we want.

But a person being shaped by the wisdom of God learns to ask:

Who can I help?
Who do I need to treat fairly?
How can I use my influence without taking advantage of others?

We need to examine not only how we see ourselves, but also what kind of effect our presence has on the lives of other people.

👉 The condition of our hearts is revealed in the way we use our words, power, and influence in the lives of others.

In Christ We See the Perfect Example

When we honestly examine ourselves, we realize that we have not always used well what God has entrusted to us. There are times when we put ourselves first and use our influence mainly to protect our own interests.

But in Jesus Christ, we see the perfect example of a life that became a blessing to others.

He possessed power, yet He did not use it for His own advantage. He had authority, yet He used it to serve. He drew near to those whom others rejected, showed compassion to those who were suffering, and welcomed sinners who came to Him in repentance.

We see this most clearly at the cross. Jesus, who was without sin, willingly gave His life for sinners. He did not use His power to spare Himself from suffering. Instead, He gave Himself so that we might be saved.

Here we also see that our own effort is not enough to change a self-centered heart. We need Christ's forgiveness and the new life that comes from Him.

Through the Holy Spirit, God gradually transforms us so that the things we once used mainly for ourselves become things we learn to use in serving others.

👉 In Christ, we see that true greatness is not found in using power for ourselves, but in using our lives to serve others.

Use Your Influence for the Good of Others

You do not have to be wealthy, famous, or powerful to influence others. Every one of us has people whose lives are affected by the way we live.

At home, we can become a source of fear and tension, or we can help create an atmosphere marked by respect, honesty, and grace.

At work, we can use our position to take advantage of others, or we can treat them fairly. We can claim all the credit for ourselves, or we can recognize the contributions of others and help them grow.

In the church, we can serve in order to gain people's admiration, or we can use our gifts to strengthen the faith of others.

Even on social media, our words can stir up anger and division, or we can use our voice with truth, wisdom, and grace.

👉 Wherever God has placed us, let us use our influence not to exalt ourselves, but to bring goodness, justice, and grace into the lives of others.

Living It Out: What Does My Life Bring to Others?

Let us examine ourselves:

  • What effect do my words, attitudes, and decisions have on the people closest to me?
  • When I have influence, do I think first about myself or about what would be good for others?
  • Is anyone being hurt or neglected because I keep putting my own desires first?
  • What ability, opportunity, position, or blessing has God entrusted to me that I can use to help others?
  • How can I follow Christ's example by using my influence to serve?

CONCLUSION

Proverbs 11:10 reminds us that our lives are not lived in isolation. Both righteousness and wickedness affect the people around us. A person who uses his life for what is good becomes a source of joy, while someone who uses power for selfish purposes can become a burden to many.

So we should not only ask,

“Am I prospering?”

We should also ask,

“What is happening to the people around me as I prosper?”

In Christ, we see the perfect example of a life given for the good of others. And in Him, we also receive forgiveness and a new heart that the Holy Spirit continues to shape.

👉 God's blessings are not meant only for our own benefit. In the hands of someone being shaped by Christ, those blessings become a means through which others can also experience goodness, justice, and grace.

The question:
If you honestly examined the effect your life has on the people around you, would you see that you are using the blessings God has entrusted to you to serve others as Christ did, or are you still using them more for yourself than for the good of others?
