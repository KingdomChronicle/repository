Daily Devotional Verses



Proverbs 10:32

NKJV (New King James Version)

The Proverbs of Solomon:

The lips of the righteous know what is acceptable, But the mouth of the wicked what is perverse.

GUIDE REFLECTION:

Wisdom Knows How to Speak in the Right Way

Not every truth should be spoken at every moment, and not every good intention is communicated in the right way.

As people, we often fail not only in what we do but also in what we say. There are words whose content may be true but whose delivery is wrong. There are also times when remaining silent is far wiser than speaking.

Solomon said:

"The lips of the righteous know what is acceptable, But the mouth of the wicked what is perverse."

This verse reveals that a righteous person does not merely speak the truth; he also knows how to speak at the right time and in a way that benefits others. His words bring hope, offer loving correction, and promote peace. In contrast, the wicked use their mouths to tear others down, stir up conflict, and spread words that destroy those around them.

👉 True wisdom is not only knowing what to say but also knowing how and when to say it.

Our Speech Is the Fruit of a Heart That Submits to God

A person may know what is good to say, but that does not necessarily mean he will choose to say it. Many people know that slander, lying, profanity, or insulting speech is wrong, yet they continue to practice these things. The root of the problem is not a lack of knowledge but a lack of obedience to God.

A person who walks in wisdom allows God to guide his heart, and as a result, he becomes careful with his words. He understands that every word can either become an instrument to heal a wounded heart or cause an even deeper wound. Therefore, he chooses to speak with humility, truth, and love.

On the other hand, a heart that rejects God's wisdom easily becomes destructive. Slander, mockery, gossip, and hurtful words become a normal part of that person's life because they simply reflect what fills the heart.

👉 Godly speech is the fruit of a heart that submits to God, not merely of good manners.

What Do Your Words Reveal?

It is easy to blame our anger, exhaustion, or circumstances whenever we say something hurtful. Yet most of the time, our words simply reveal what truly fills our hearts. If our mouths are constantly filled with complaints, slander, pride, or contempt for others, they may be exposing areas of our hearts that have not yet been fully surrendered to the Lord.

Today, harsh words, insulting jokes, and speech that tears down the dignity of others have become normal for many people. But God's standard never changes. He calls His children to be different in the way they speak so that His wisdom and holiness may be seen through them.

👉 The way we speak reveals who truly reigns in our hearts.

In Christ We Learn to Speak with Grace and Truth

All of us have fallen short in our speech. We have spoken words that wounded others, made false accusations, or uttered things that were displeasing to God. We cannot transform ourselves through self-discipline alone.

But Jesus Christ is the perfect example of holy speech. His words are full of truth and grace. He knew how to correct with love, comfort those who were suffering, and give hope to the repentant. No deceit or slander ever came from His mouth.

Through His death and resurrection, He forgave our sins and gave us new hearts. As we continue to walk with Him, the Holy Spirit gradually transforms our speech so that it becomes an instrument of truth, grace, and love. A person whom Christ is transforming does not merely guard his words; he also learns to use them to make God known to others.

👉 Only through Christ can we receive a heart that is able to speak the truth with love and grace.

Use Your Words to Give Life

Every day God gives us opportunities to use our mouths as a blessing. At home, choose to speak with respect. At work, be honest and gentle. In the church, encourage your brothers and sisters in Christ. On social media, be careful that every word you share promotes truth rather than conflict.

We cannot control everything we experience, but we can choose for our speech to reflect the wisdom of God. When Christ reigns in our hearts, our words become a means through which others can see His goodness.

👉 A mouth ruled by Christ becomes an instrument of peace, truth, and blessing.

Living It Out: What Does My Speech Reveal?

  • Do my words usually bring hope and blessing to others?
  • Are there times when I use my mouth for slander, gossip, or hurtful speech?
  • How can I speak the truth without losing love and humility?
  • Do my family and those around me see Christ's transforming work in the way I speak?
  • What does God want to change in my heart so that my words will become more pleasing to Him?

CONCLUSION

Proverbs teaches that the speech of the righteous is always fitting because it flows from a heart filled with wisdom. In contrast, the speech of the wicked becomes an instrument of destruction because it springs from a heart that is drifting away from God.

In Jesus Christ we find both the perfect example of holy speech and the power to transform our hearts. As we continue to follow Him, He shapes our words into instruments of His grace and truth. May every word that comes from our mouths become a reason for others to know and glorify God more fully.

👉 A person who truly walks with Christ is recognized by life-giving speech, not by speech that tears others down.

The question:

If the people around you and the members of your family listened to your words every day, would they see the wisdom, grace, and truth of Jesus Christ reigning in your heart, or are there still areas that God needs to transform so that your speech may truly become a blessing to them?
