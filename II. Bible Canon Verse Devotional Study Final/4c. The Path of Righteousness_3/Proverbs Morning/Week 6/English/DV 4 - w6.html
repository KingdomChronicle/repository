Daily Devotional Verses



Proverbs 11:2

NKJV (New King James Version)

The Folly of Wickedness

When pride comes, then comes shame; But with the humble is wisdom.

GUIDE REFLECTION:

True Wisdom Begins with Humility

In today's world, thinking highly of oneself is often praised and encouraged. Many people believe that the secret to success is having complete confidence in our abilities and believing that we can determine our own future. Yet the Word of God teaches us a different truth. True wisdom begins not with lifting ourselves up but with humbling ourselves before the Lord.

Solomon declared:

"When pride comes, then comes shame; But with the humble is wisdom."

Pride is more than simply displaying arrogance. It is a mindset that places confidence in oneself instead of depending on God. A proud person begins to believe that he no longer needs the Lord's direction. He thinks that his knowledge, abilities, or achievements are sufficient. But Scripture consistently reveals that this kind of attitude eventually leads to failure and disgrace.

In contrast, a humble person understands his limitations and relies on God's wisdom. Because of this, he becomes more willing to learn, accept correction, and follow the will of the Lord.

👉 Humility is not a sign of weakness but an acknowledgment that God alone is the true source of wisdom.

Pride Draws a Person Away from God

When pride fills the heart, dependence upon God slowly fades away. A proud person can easily begin believing that he controls everything. When he succeeds, he gives himself all the credit. When he fails, he is often quick to blame others for what went wrong.

We see the danger of this kind of heart in the life of King Herod. He received praise that belonged only to God and failed to give the Lord the glory He deserved. This is what we read in Acts:

King Herod Agrippa I put on his royal apparel and addressed the people. The people shouted:

“The voice of a god and not of a man!”

He did not reject or correct their praise. Because he did not give glory to God, an angel of the Lord struck him, and he died.

Acts 12:21–23

Herod neither corrected the praise of the people nor gave the glory back to God. Instead, he accepted honor that no human being deserved. His downfall clearly reminds us that pride pulls a person away from God and can eventually result in shame and destruction.

This does not mean that recognizing the blessings, abilities, or accomplishments God has given us is wrong. The problem arises when we take for ourselves the glory that rightfully belongs to the Lord. When we forget that everything we have comes from Him, we can easily begin relying on ourselves instead of trusting God.

A humble person, however, sees things differently. He understands that every achievement is a blessing from God. Therefore, rather than boasting about himself, he directs all praise to the Lord and continually seeks His guidance.

Humility means recognizing that we are not the source of our wisdom, abilities, or accomplishments. God is the One who provides all these things, and therefore He alone deserves all the glory.

👉 A proud heart depends on itself, while a humble heart depends on God and returns all glory to Him.

What Is the True Condition of Your Heart?

We can easily notice pride in other people but often struggle to recognize it within ourselves. It may be revealed in many ways—refusing to acknowledge a mistake, resisting the need to apologize, insisting on always being right, or longing for the approval of others. At times, it can quietly remain in our hearts without us even noticing it.

Humility, on the other hand, does not mean having a low view of ourselves. It means seeing ourselves correctly in light of who God is. We recognize that we possess gifts and abilities, while also understanding that the Lord is the One who has given them to us. Therefore, we do not pursue our own glory but seek to honor God.

👉 A genuinely humble heart is ready to accept correction, continue learning, and give glory to God.

In Christ We See the Perfect Example of Humility

Jesus Christ is the ultimate example of genuine humility. Though He is the Son of God, He did not demand to use His glory for His personal benefit. Instead, He humbled Himself, took on human form, and remained obedient even unto death on the cross to rescue us from our sins.

Through His sacrifice, He demonstrated that true greatness is not determined by authority or popularity but by humble obedience to the Father. Because He humbled Himself, God exalted Him greatly and gave Him the name above every name.

As we continue walking with Christ, He also transforms our hearts little by little. He teaches us to lay down our pride, receive His grace, and walk in humility. True wisdom is not found in lifting ourselves up but in following Christ's example.

👉 In Christ we discover that the way of humility is the way that leads to genuine wisdom.

Live with Humility Every Day

Humility is revealed not only through important choices but also through the ordinary moments of daily life. It is shown when we are willing to listen, accept counsel, apologize when we are wrong, and serve without looking for recognition. A humble person does not strive to appear great before others but desires to be pleasing in God's sight.

In a world surrounded by competition and pride, a humble heart becomes a powerful testimony to the gospel. As people observe our humility, they can also see more clearly the character of Christ at work in our lives.

👉 Humility that comes from God's grace becomes a testimony of His wisdom working in our lives.

Living It Out: Am I Living with Humility?

  • Are there parts of my life where I depend on myself more than I depend on God?
  • How do I react when someone corrects or rebukes me?
  • Am I willing to apologize quickly and acknowledge my mistakes?
  • Do my achievements point the glory toward God or toward myself?
  • How can I show the humility of Christ in my home, workplace, and ministry?

CONCLUSION

Proverbs teaches us that pride can never produce genuine success. It may give us temporary admiration from people, but ultimately, it brings shame. Humility, however, makes room for God's wisdom and for the life He chooses to bless.

In Jesus Christ, we see the perfect model of humility and obedience to the Father. As we keep following Him, He changes our hearts by taking away pride and replacing it with true wisdom. May we choose the way of humility every day, because there we experience more deeply the grace and direction of God.

👉 A truly wise person is not one who considers himself highly but one who humbly relies on God in every aspect of life.

The question:

If God were to examine your heart today, would He see that you are depending on your own abilities, or would He find a humble heart willing to obey and give all glory to Jesus Christ?
