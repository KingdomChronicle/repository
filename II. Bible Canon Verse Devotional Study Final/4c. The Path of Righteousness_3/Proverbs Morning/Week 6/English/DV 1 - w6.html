Daily Devotional Verses



Proverbs 10:31

NKJV (New King James Version)

The proverbs of Solomon

The mouth of the righteous brings forth wisdom, But the perverse tongue will be cut out.

GUIDE REFLECTION:

Speech Reveals the Condition of the Heart

Every day we speak. At home, at work, at school, in church, and even on social media, our words have the power to strengthen or destroy, to bring hope or inflict wounds. We often think that our speech simply comes out of our mouths, but Solomon reminds us that our words reveal the true condition of our hearts.

Solomon said:

"The mouth of the righteous brings forth wisdom, but the perverse tongue will be cut out."

This verse does not merely teach that there is a difference between good and evil speech. It also shows that God's wisdom is revealed through the way we speak. A person who walks in righteousness becomes a source of truth, sound counsel, encouragement, and words that lead others to God. On the other hand, a person who lives in falsehood gradually exposes the true condition of his own heart through his words.

👉 Our speech reveals not only what we think but also the true condition of our hearts before God.

Wisdom Is Revealed Through the Truth We Speak

True wisdom is not measured by how much knowledge we possess or how eloquently we speak. Wisdom that comes from God begins with reverence for Him and obedience to Him. When the heart is filled with His Word, the mouth learns to speak truth, love, and grace.

A righteous person becomes a blessing to others. He gives sound counsel to those who are confused, encourages the discouraged, comforts those who are suffering, and gently corrects those who have gone astray. His words are not used to exalt himself or to wound others but to build people up and glorify God.

In contrast, a person who does not acknowledge God is quick to use his mouth for deception, slander, profanity, gossip, or the distortion of truth for personal gain. But Solomon's warning is clear: such speech will not endure. The day will come when God will hold every person accountable for every word they have spoken.

👉 A heart filled with wisdom naturally speaks the truth and becomes a blessing to others.

What Do Your Words Reveal?

We often say, "I didn't mean it," or, "It just slipped out." But Jesus also taught that the mouth speaks from what fills the heart. When the heart is filled with anger, envy, pride, or deceit, these things will eventually be revealed through our speech.

Before we try to change our words, we must first allow God to examine our hearts. For the problem is not merely improper speech but the sinful heart from which it flows. A transformed tongue begins with a transformed heart.

👉 The transformation of our speech begins when we allow God to transform our hearts.

In Christ We Find the Truth That Transforms the Heart

All of us have sinned with our words. There have been times when we have lied, hurt others, judged people, or spoken without love. Because of this, none of us has any righteousness to boast of before God.

But Jesus Christ came, the One who is "the way, the truth, and the life." No deceit ever came from His mouth. Every word He spoke declared truth, grace, and life. On the cross, He bore the punishment for our sins so that everyone who believes in Him may receive forgiveness and a new heart.

When Christ reigns in our lives, the Holy Spirit gradually transforms our speech as well. The mouth once used for slander learns to forgive. The tongue that once spoke lies learns to speak the truth in love. And words that were once empty become instruments for making God's wisdom and goodness known to others.

👉 In Christ begins the true transformation of the heart, and from there our speech is transformed as well.

Use Your Mouth as an Instrument of Blessing

Every day, we have the opportunity to choose how we will use our words. We can either become the cause of another person's discouragement or be a channel of hope and truth.

In our homes, let us speak carefully to our family members.

At work, let us speak with integrity.

In the church, let us use our mouths to encourage others and teach the truth.

And in every conversation, let us remember that our words reflect our relationship with Christ.

👉 A mouth ruled by Christ becomes an instrument of wisdom, truth, and blessing.

Living It Out: What Does My Speech Reveal?

  • Do my words generally bring life and wisdom to others?
  • Are there lies, slander, or hurtful words that I need to confess to God?
  • Do my family and those around me see the transformation of my heart through the way I speak?
  • How can I use my mouth to proclaim the truth and love of Christ more faithfully?
  • What area of my heart does God desire to transform so that my speech may also become holy?

CONCLUSION

Proverbs teaches that our speech is not a small matter in God's sight. The words we speak may be the fruit of wisdom or the fruit of a heart that is drifting away from Him. The day will come when God will hold every person accountable for their words.

But in Jesus Christ there is hope for every repentant heart. He is the Truth who forgives, transforms, and gives new life. As we walk with Him, He gradually shapes our hearts so that every word that comes from our mouths may become an instrument of His grace and truth.

👉 A person transformed by Christ is recognized not only by what they do but also by the words that come from their mouth.

The question:

If people were to listen to your words every day, would they see the wisdom and truth of Jesus Christ reigning in your heart, or do your words reveal a heart that still needs to be fully surrendered to Him?
