Daily Devotional Verses



Proverbs 10:25

NKJV (New King James Version)

The Proverbs of Solomon:

When the whirlwind passes by, the wicked is no more, But the righteous has an everlasting foundation.

GUIDE REFLECTION:

A Strong Life Has a Strong Foundation

All of us will face the storms of life. There are trials that come unexpectedly—illness, the loss of a loved one, failure at work, family struggles, or seasons when it seems that every problem comes all at once.

In times like these, a person's strength is not measured by physical ability, intelligence, or wealth. It is during such moments that the true foundation of one's life is revealed.

Solomon said:

"The wicked is no more when the whirlwind passes by, but the righteous has an everlasting foundation."

This verse presents two different kinds of foundations. A person who lives apart from God may appear secure while everything is calm, but when the storm arrives, the things he depends on quickly collapse. In contrast, the person who lives in righteousness remains steadfast because his life is rooted in God.

👉 A strong life is not measured by the absence of storms, but by remaining standing in the midst of them.

Obedience to God Strengthens Our Lives

Proverbs does not promise that the righteous will never experience trouble. Even God's servants went through severe trials.

Job lost almost everything.

Joseph was sold as a slave and imprisoned.

David spent many years fleeing from his enemies.

The apostles endured persecution.

Yet through all these things, they were not completely destroyed because their trust was in the Lord.

On the other hand, the person who trusts only in personal ability, wealth, power, or sinful pursuits has no firm support when those things are taken away.

👉 Obedience to God does not remove the storm, but it gives a stability that no storm can destroy.

The Things of This World Are Not a Firm Foundation

Many people build their lives on temporary things.

They believe money is their security.

Their job is their identity.

Their health is their strength.

Their relationships are their hope.

But all these things can disappear in an instant.

When our hearts rely only on these things, our peace can easily disappear as well.

But God never changes.

His faithfulness does not depend on our circumstances.

Even if the whole world changes, He remains faithful to His children.

👉 Any foundation that is not built upon God is only temporary.

Christ Is the Only Sure Foundation

Proverbs repeatedly teaches that true wisdom comes from the Lord. This wisdom has been fully revealed in Jesus Christ.

Jesus Himself taught this truth in the parable of the two builders.

The person who hears and obeys His words is like a man who built his house upon the rock.

When the rain, floods, and strong winds came, the house did not fall because its foundation was secure.

But the one who built on the sand collapsed when the storm came.

More than that, the greatest storm every person will face is not merely the troubles of this world but the holy judgment of God against sin.

We are powerless to save ourselves.

But Jesus Christ came to bear our sins on the cross. Through His death and resurrection, He has given us forgiveness, new life, and the assurance that we are saved from God's judgment.

When Christ is the foundation of our lives, trials may shake us, but they cannot ultimately destroy us because our salvation and hope rest in Him, not in our own strength.

👉 The strongest foundation of life is not religion, personal ability, or good works, but Jesus Christ alone.

Remain Rooted in Christ Every Day

Strong faith is not developed only during times of crisis.

It is cultivated through daily immersion to the Word of God, prayer, obedience, and trust in Him.

The more we come to know Christ, the deeper our roots grow.

And the deeper our roots grow in Him, the more steadfast we become in the face of every storm.

So do not wait until the storm comes before strengthening your faith.

Begin today by continually building your life upon the Lord.

👉 The person who walks daily with Christ will be prepared for the storms that lie ahead.

Living It Out: What Is the Foundation of My Life?

Examine yourself:

  • When trials come, where do I first turn—to my own ability or to the Lord?
  • Are there things I have made my primary foundation apart from Christ?
  • Are my decisions based on the Word of God or merely on what is comfortable?
  • How can I deepen my relationship with Christ each day?
  • If a severe trial came today, what would keep my faith standing firm?

CONCLUSION

The Proverbs of King Solomon remind us that storms are a part of every person's life. The difference is not who experiences the storm, but who remains standing after it has passed.

The person who trusts in personal strength, wealth, or the things of this world is easily swept away when trials come. But the person who trusts in Christ has a foundation that no storm can destroy.

Our true security is not found in our circumstances but in our relationship with the Lord. He alone is the Rock that cannot be shaken, the Savior who never changes, and the hope that endures forever.

👉 The storms of life may shake the person who is rooted in Christ, but his faith will never be overcome, because God is his firm foundation.

The question:

When the storms of life come and on the day you stand before the Lord, will it be evident that your life is built upon the firm foundation of Jesus Christ, or are there still areas of your life resting on things that can easily be swept away by the wind?
